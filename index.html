Privacy Policy

Gymscle · Last updated: June 16, 2026

Gymscle is a workout tracker. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. The short version: the app keeps your data on your own device and in your private iCloud, and the developer does not collect, see, or share any of it.

What data the app stores

Gymscle stores the information you create while using it, such as:

  • Training plans, workouts, and your training history
  • Personal records, goals, and progress statistics
  • App preferences (your name or nickname, accent color, light or dark appearance, units, and notification settings)

Where your data is stored

All of this data is stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the data also syncs to your own private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) so it is available across your devices. This iCloud data lives in your personal account and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer of Gymscle has no access to your iCloud data.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Gymscle reads your heart rate, calories, and workout duration from workouts you recorded (for example, on an Apple Watch) so it can show them next to your matching sessions in your workout history. Gymscle only reads this data; it never writes anything to Apple Health.

This health data is read and shown only on your device. Unlike the rest of your data, it is not synced to iCloud and is not stored anywhere outside your device. It is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing. You can turn this access on or off at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Health.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Gymscle has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. There is no Gymscle server. Your data is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Notifications

If you enable them, the app schedules local notifications on your device (rest timer alerts and an optional daily reminder). These are generated on your device and are not sent through any external service for tracking purposes.

Links to other websites

The app may open external web pages (for example, exercise source links) in an in-app browser. Those websites are operated by others and have their own privacy policies.

Importing and exporting your data

You can export your training history to a file or a code, and import it back, entirely under your control. These files are created and shared only when you choose to, using your device's standard share and file tools.

Deleting your data

You control your data. You can delete individual items in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app. Data stored in iCloud can be removed through your device's iCloud settings.

Children

Gymscle is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
